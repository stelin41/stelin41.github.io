Novel discovery about “Cicada 3301”, 13 years after (2025 update)

0001-01-01
#cicada 3301  #research 

Cicada 3301⌗

If you’re reading this you probably already heared about Cicada 3301 (if not, google it). Surprisingly, it was not publicly known what the name nor the number “Cicada 3301” means, until now.

Discovery⌗

I’ll go straigt to the point. Yotsuba&! page 33, volume 01.

Claim: Cicada 3301 is a reference to this page.

Evidence:

  • Page 33, volume 01, Yotsuba literally saying Cicada.
  • Yotsuba is also the mascot of 4chan (a.k.a. 404 girl), the site where Cicada 3301 made their first post.
  • Cicada 3301 used books in their puzzles multiple times.

Additionally, for some puzzles they sticked their Cicada symbol in poles (?). This one might not count as evidence.

How did I find it?⌗

Simple: I decided to check Yotsuba&! during my break out of curiosity, to see if I would like the manga. I already knew that Yotsuba was chosen as the mascot of 4chan, but when I read “Cicada” it rang a bell, which made me notice the page and volume number. That’s it. I’m surprised that it kept unnoticed for 13 years.
